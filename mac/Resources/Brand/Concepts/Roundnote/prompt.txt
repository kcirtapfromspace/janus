Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one flat identity concept sheet for a native Mac interview review and preparation app.
Brand name (verbatim): "roundnote". R-o-u-n-d-n-o-t-e, one word, no other text.
Product character: a private interview notebook that helps someone learn from real conversations and prepare for their next interview round. Thoughtful, capable, human. The current interface is editorial, with warm paper, Georgia headings, quiet rules, and restrained forest green. This is a logo proposal.
Create one original, highly resolved symbol and wordmark. Symbol: a compact sculpted capital R with a distinctive open counter that feels like a speaking turn or quotation. A firm vertical stem, an open rounded upper conversation contour, and a short deliberate diagonal leg; simple enough to redraw as a few vector paths and recognize at 24 pixels. Balanced negative space. Give the geometry an individual slightly pen-cut character, without sketchy strokes. Do not combine many literal objects. The name should read in an excellent moderately weighted editorial serif, humane proportions, beautiful deliberate kerning, not delicate luxury fashion lettering, not a handwritten font.
Palette: forest green #345B49, warm paper #F4F0E7, ink #2B3029. Dark presentation: background #24251F and pale sage #B2CAB7.
Composition: a crisp landscape identity sheet. Upper two thirds is warm paper with a single large horizontal logo lockup and ample breathing room. Lower third is dark with the identical logo reversed into pale sage, a small simple Mac app icon application of the same symbol on a paper tile, and the symbol on its own at small scale. Keep clear alignment and print-design restraint. The exact same symbol in every use. All elements are flat digital artwork with sharp edges, suitable as reference for later vector production.
Avoid: gradients, shadows, glass, sparkles, stars, brains, circuits, robot motifs, microphones, swooshes, arrows, charts, generic growth chevrons, blobs, mockup photography, stationery props, slogans, badges, overly rounded presentation cards, repeated headings, watermark. No extra copy. The only text is "roundnote" in the two wordmarks.
