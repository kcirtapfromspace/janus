Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE polished landscape identity exploration sheet for the product "Janus", an interview reflection app that helps people find useful feedback when a hiring process is silent or ghosts them. The central brand idea is duality: looking backward for understanding, looking forward with agency. Human, thoughtful, a little uncompromising. This is a serious designer's mark exploration, not a SaaS branding template.

Layout: six equally sized specimens on a warm ivory background, a precise 3-column by 2-row grid with very faint rules, generous negative space. Each cell shows a single strong black logo mark large in its upper half, then a carefully kerned "Janus" wordmark beneath, and a discreet small caption with its number and name. Also include one very small black square at the bottom right of each cell showing the same mark reversed in ivory at app-icon size. Do not use rounded presentation cards, mockups, perspective, shadows, gradients, texture or decorative illustrations. Extremely clean vector-like edges. The marks must be genuinely different silhouettes and ideas, not small variants of one shape.

The six specified directions:
"01  Threshold": an architectural glyph, two asymmetric solid pillars framing an unmistakably open central passage; a small break or displacement implies a transition from one side to the other. Ancient doorway translated into a compact modern mark. Flat bold and sculptural, no literal building illustration. Pair with an elegant but sturdy humanist serif Janus.
"02  Counterform": a single memorable monogram built from two opposed, specially drawn J forms; sophisticated positive/negative-space interplay. They do not become an infinity sign, a chain link or a heart. Feels like a typesetter's emblem. Pair with a restrained contemporary serif Janus.
"03  Reply": a distinctive compact typographic symbol based on two opposing quotation/answer strokes or brackets, with a clear pause between them. One forward gesture, one returning gesture. Unexpected, asymmetrical, spare, no speech bubble, no chat icon, no Wi-Fi arcs. Pair with a warm humanist sans Janus.
"04  Parallax": two offset angular planes that reveal a clear central slit by their overlap; perception changes between two views. Deliberate distinctive cut geometry, balanced as a small icon. Do not create a stock chevron, cube, hexagon, eye or diamond. Pair with a quiet grotesk Janus.
"05  Letterform": custom logotype-only Janus with a remarkable J, subtle split terminal or reverse curve alluding to two perspectives. Editorial, highly legible, unusual letter detail, mature and compact. The large sample is the complete wordmark; the small black icon uses its custom J alone. Do not add a detached symbol. Use title case exactly Janus.
"06  Hinge": a compact circular seal containing a strong off-center vertical hinge and two different solid semicircular gestures turning in opposite directions. Philosophical, kinetic, timeless ink-stamp character through geometry alone; no distress. No yin-yang, arrows, faces, laurel, stars or astrology. Pair with a substantial old-style serif Janus.

Typography captions small, simple neutral sans, exact labels above. No headings, slogans or paragraphs on the sheet. All six must be optical-size friendly: bold enough for 20-pixel menu bar use and monochrome reproduction. NO human face silhouettes, paired heads, robot heads, brains, generic AI sparkles, circuit boards, gradients, glossy 3D icons, generic infinity signs or abstract corporate swooshes. Treat each as an original professional identity concept.
