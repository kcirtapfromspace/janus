# Janus 0.1.0-preview.16

- **Practice interviews.** Choose **Practice Interview** in the menu bar, or **Practice** in the window. An interviewer asks real questions out loud: first ones from your past interviews (the company and role you name, then the answers you found hardest), then questions other Janus users have shared, then classic ones. It follows up like a real interviewer, and the session is reviewed like any interview. Practice is tagged as such and kept out of your interview counts. Headphones give the clearest recording.
- **Correct the video.** Each video note in a review has "Not what you saw?", and the Review tab has **Correct video cues…**. Your corrections help measure how accurate the video cues are.
- **More accurate video cues.** Your own head movements are no longer counted as the interviewer's nods when your face drops out of view. A still picture is no longer treated as missing video. A recording that was cut short keeps the faces read up to that point. The video section stays marked experimental until its cues pass testing against labelled calls.
- **What Janus shares.** A new window explains it once, with a switch for each:
  - **Your interviews' questions:** rewritten to name no person, company or product before they're shared, so others can practise with them once approved. Never your answers, transcripts, audio or video. You can withdraw what you've shared in Settings.
  - **Anonymous diagnostics:** whether recording, reviews and practice worked, never content.

  Both are on by default, and nothing is sent until you've seen the window.
- For the command line: `ic questions`, `ic eval scorers --set video`, `ic eval video-health`, and `ic registry`.

Existing interviews, settings, sign-ins, and automatic updates carry forward. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon.
