# Janus 0.1.0-preview.20

- **Arrange your library.** Drag companies, the roles at a company, and a role's rounds into the order you want; Janus keeps it. New companies and roles arrive at the top and new rounds at the end of their role. **Reset to Date Order** in the library's filter menu puts everything back.
- **Add a round from the library.** Hover a role and click **+** to record a new round straight into it, or import a recording as one. The Record window shows which role the round is added to. A company's **+** records or imports an interview at that company.
- **Archive or delete a whole company or role.** A company's or role's **⋯** menu, also on right-click, now has **Delete**, alongside Rename and Archive. Archiving hides the group until **Show archived** is on. Deleting moves its interviews to Recently Deleted for 30 days, and restoring any one of them brings its role back. Companies can now be collapsed, like roles.

Your library upgrades itself once, the first time this version opens it. Existing interviews, settings, sign-ins, and automatic updates carry forward. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon.
