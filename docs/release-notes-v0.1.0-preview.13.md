# Janus 0.1.0-preview.13

- Interview Coach is now Janus, with Roman profiles formed by opposing quotation marks across the app icon, menu bar, and reviews.
- A refreshed interview notebook brings your latest conversation, earlier interviews, and local analytics into one view. Recorded outcomes stay separate from predicted verdicts.
- Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance. System is the default, and open reviews follow appearance changes immediately.
- Completed interviews open in Review. Imported interviews are selected after processing, and returning to the notebook stops audio playback.
- Saved and newly generated reviews share the new typography, colors, and Janus identity.
- Recording starts only after explicit consent, with clearer setup, progress, and retry controls.

Existing interviews, settings, sign-ins, and automatic updates carry forward. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon.
