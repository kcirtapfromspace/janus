# Janus 0.1.0-preview.21

- **See how you're doing across interviews.** The top of the interview notebook now has **How you're doing**. It opens with a sentence on where your overall score is heading and a chart of it, interview by interview. Below that, every area the reviews measure is marked **Getting better**, **Slipping** or **Steady**: the rubric, your answer habits, how you speak, and the room. Your strongest and weakest areas lately, and the coaching that keeps coming up, sit alongside. It updates as soon as a new review finishes and follows the notebook's **30 days**, **90 days** and **All time** picker.
- **Directions that hold up.** An area needs three reviewed interviews before it shows a direction. Your latest interviews are compared with the ones before, and small changes count as steady, so one unusual review doesn't make a trend. Practice, archived and deleted interviews are left out, and a recording with fewer than 100 of your words doesn't count toward how you speak.
- **`ic trends` in Terminal.** It prints the same summary. `--days N` limits it to recent interviews, and `--json` gives the data.

Existing interviews, settings, sign-ins, and automatic updates carry forward. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon.
